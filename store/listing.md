# Google Play: the store listing

Hebrew is the default language of the listing (the families are in Israel; the parents' area is Hebrew), English the
translation. Chacho speaks: he is a he (צ׳אצ׳ו אומר, אני תוכי). No emoji, no "best" or "number 1", no claim the store app
does not do (the Nest is not in it; no AI talks with the kid; nothing is sold). Limits: title 30 characters, short
description 80, full description 4,000 (lengths in brackets; the full texts are 1,661 and 2,226 characters).

When something changes (sign-up opens to everyone, a paid plan, the Nest joins the app), change this text in the same commit.

## Full description, English

```
Hi, I'm Chacho, a parrot who loves English. Every day I have a short mission waiting for you, about ten minutes, right at your level.

How it works
- A short level test inside the app finds where to start, from A1 up to C1.
- A new mission every day, in five parts: a hook, learn, practice, use it, and finish. There are grammar labs, word packs, a recorded radio show, story cards, a newsroom and a writer's desk.
- Four quick games: Match it, Hear it, Build it and Spot it. Words you missed come back a few days later, until they stick.
- Talk with me: I speak with a recorded voice, and you answer with sentence buttons, or out loud if your parents turned the microphone on.

What waits for you on the way
- A pet of your own, one of ten, that grows through five stages as you practice.
- A map with a world for every level, a station for every day, and a gate at the end of each world: pass a short challenge and move up a level.
- My shop: buy clothes for your pet with coins you earn by practicing, and ask me for them in English. In the Studio you describe an item in English and watch it being made.
- Play with your brothers, sisters and friends: Word Duel, Challenge, a Boss Battle against the Word Thief, Tug of War on one phone, and a describing game in pairs. Only preset messages and reactions, no chat.
- Hebrew help for levels A1 and A2: a small line in Hebrew under the instructions.

For parents
- A parents' area in Hebrew: what your child did every day, scores and streak, and an evening summary notification with a question for the dinner table.
- You choose: one reminder a day at your hour or none, the microphone for each child, and playing with other families (off until both families turn it on).
- No ads, no purchases and no loot boxes. Coins are only earned by practicing.
- We keep only what is needed to teach. You can download all the data, or delete a child or the whole family, inside the app.
- The content is written for each level and checked step by step by an English teacher. Found a mistake? Report it on any question.

For children from Israeli grade 3 (about age 8). During the pilot, families join with an invitation link, and the app is free.

See you at tomorrow's mission,
Chacho
```

Before publishing, check each line against the build once more: the dinner question (parents' 21:00 summary), the five play
modes (Play tab), the Studio (open since `Settings.ShopOpens`), the level test (new kids, `Placements`).
